Konfidens, a practice management platform for mental health professionals in Norway and the UK, chose Whereby for its native European GDPR understanding, embedded integration (built in about 48 hours), and flexible per-minute pricing that enabled pay-per-call rates. The platform now serves 500-600 practices and over 1,000 practitioners with features including local session recording and customizable meeting interfaces.
