Ten technical insights to guide developers when choosing or evaluating a video call API to make integrated calls feel native. The recommendations cover infrastructure maintenance costs, vendor stability, media architecture options (P2P, SFU, MCU), security compliance, observability, UI customization, pre-call device checks, AI readiness, webhook reliability, and mobile integration, stressing that the real test begins months post-deployment during compliance audits and on legacy devices or poor networks.
