Whereby has partnered with Open Path Collective, a nonprofit supporting over 35,000 therapists across the US and Canada. Starting in January 2026, therapists on Open Path's annual plans gain access to Whereby Meetings with free HIPAA compliance add-ons, featuring secure lockable rooms, calendar integrations, and no downloads for clients.
