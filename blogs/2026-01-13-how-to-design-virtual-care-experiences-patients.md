Virtual care requires its own design principles, with technology that fades into the background so the clinical interaction takes center stage. The article highlights three core priorities - simplicity, transparency, and accessibility - supported by features like pre-call checks, in-call diagnostics, live captions, and minimal toolbars, and notes that trust translates into reduced no-shows, higher repeat usage, and fewer support tickets.
